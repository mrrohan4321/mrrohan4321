### 🚀 Projects

#### ✈️ AI Travel Intelligence
An AI trip planner for Indian domestic travel. Give it a destination, budget, dates, group size and travel style, and it researches the place live (SerpApi), checks real weather (Open-Meteo) and road distances (Nominatim + OSRM), prices the whole trip line by line with a deterministic cost engine, and writes a day-by-day itinerary plus a PDF to carry on the road. Has an instant "What if?" mode to re-plan on a different budget, headcount or transport. Built entirely on free tiers.

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repo-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/mrrohan4321/ai-travel-intelligence)
[![Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Demo-FF0000?style=for-the-badge&logo=youtube&logoColor=white)](https://youtu.be/anAh1D-6G3k)

#### ❤️ ECG Signal Classification
Group 7 deep learning project — classifies ECG heartbeats as Normal or Abnormal with a **1D-CNN** and a **CNN-LSTM** on the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database, and benchmarks **GPU vs CPU** speed for training and inference. Comes with a live Streamlit demo. Educational project, not a clinical diagnosis.

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repo-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/mrrohan4321/ECG-Project-Deeplearning)
[![Live App](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_App-FF4B4B?style=for-the-badge&logo=streamlit&logoColor=white)](https://ecg-project-deeplearning-6383kyappwquwvbrmgodag6.streamlit.app/)
[![Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Demo-FF0000?style=for-the-badge&logo=youtube&logoColor=white)](https://youtu.be/or2yRLIMrqw)

#### ⚡ AB Electricals Website
Full storefront site for our family showroom — built from scratch in plain HTML/CSS/JS, deployed via Git/Netlify, with a custom warm amber theme, cursor-reactive spotlight, animated UI touches and embedded Google Reviews. Showcases the brands we carry (Havells, Bajaj, Maru, Diamond Lights).

[![Live Site](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Site-FF9800?style=for-the-badge&logo=netlify&logoColor=white)](https://ab-electrical.netlify.app/)
[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repo-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/mrrohan4321/ab-electricals-website)

#### 🎵 Safar
An ad-free, installable music player — streams through YouTube's own embedded player with no extra ads or tracking, built around the idea that every road has a song of its own (*প্রতিটা রাস্তার নিজের একটা গান থাকে*). Installable PWA with queue view, playlist switcher, keyboard shortcuts and lock-screen media controls, all in a single `index.html`.

[![Live Site](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Site-E91E63?style=for-the-badge&logo=netlify&logoColor=white)](https://safar-adfree.netlify.app/)
[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repo-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/mrrohan4321/safar-site)

#### 🔫 Keystroke Gunshot
A system-wide desktop app in Python (pynput + pygame) that plays a gunshot sound on every keystroke in any app. The sound is synthesised in code, so no audio file is needed, and it never records or sends what you type.

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repo-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/mrrohan4321/keystroke-gunshot)
